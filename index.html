SERVICES

Four ways to work together.

Every engagement starts with defined scope, defined deliverables, and a defined end date. No open-ended hours. No "transformation" packages. You buy a diagnostic or a build, and the deliverables are written into the contract before you sign anything.

01
Diagnostic · 2–3 weeks

RevOps Audit

A full inspection of your revenue operations stack. What's working, what's silently broken, what's costing you reporting integrity. You get a written report — not a slide deck — and a prioritized remediation roadmap you can execute with your internal team or with us.

Best for you if: your CRM looks technically correct but you don't trust the numbers it produces, your pipeline stages don't match how deals actually move, or your team has stopped maintaining properties because nobody knows what they mean anymore.

What you get
  • Property and field audit
  • Pipeline stage assessment
  • Lifecycle stage architecture review
  • Reporting integrity check
  • Workflow and automation audit
  • Prioritized remediation roadmap
02
Diagnostic · 2 weeks

AI-Readiness Audit

Your CRM is the substrate. If it's full of decay, any AI tool you layer on top — agents, scoring models, attribution engines, copilots — will produce confident garbage at scale. This audit measures the gap between where your data is and where it needs to be for AI applications to be trustworthy.

Best for you if: you're evaluating AI vendors, building internal agents, or being asked by leadership to "do something with AI." This tells you whether you can, what you need to fix first, and which use cases are actually viable for your data today.

What you get
  • Data quality scorecard
  • Taxonomy and classification review
  • Property architecture audit
  • Identity resolution assessment
  • AI use-case viability matrix
  • Foundation roadmap
03
Execution · 1–4 weeks

Implementation Sprints

You already know what's broken. You need someone to build the fix without a six-month engagement contract. Implementation sprints are sized so the deliverable lands and the engagement closes — no scope creep, no surprise retainers.

Best for you if: you've done a discovery exercise already (with us or anyone else) and you need senior hands to execute. Common projects: multi-touch attribution models, pipeline redesigns with revised forecast probabilities, industry taxonomy rollouts, marketing activity tracking, dashboard suites for sales leadership.

Common sprints
  • Multi-touch attribution build
  • Pipeline redesign and migration
  • Taxonomy / industry classification
  • Custom property architecture
  • Lifecycle stage rebuild
  • Reporting dashboard suite
04
Ongoing · Monthly

Fractional RevOps

You need a senior RevOps operator on call but you can't justify — or don't need — a full-time hire. Capped hours per month, written scope, stage-gated milestones. You always know what you're paying for and what's getting done.

Best for you if: you're founder-led and between RevOps maturity stages, or your existing RevOps lead is overloaded with day-to-day operations and needs senior strategic backup on demand.

Scope
  • CRM administration
  • Reporting maintenance
  • Workflow design and updates
  • Data governance oversight
  • Quarterly stack reviews
  • Strategic advisory on call

HOW IT WORKS

From first call to delivered work — what to expect.

A predictable process you can plan around. Every engagement follows the same shape: short discovery, scoped proposal, structured delivery, clean close. No surprises, no scope creep, no mystery deliverables.

Discovery call

A 30-minute conversation. You describe your stack, your team, and what's not working. I ask the questions that surface what the real problem is — which is often different from what you came in thinking it was.

At the end of the call you'll have a clear sense of whether this practice can help. If it can't, I'll tell you directly and point you somewhere that can.

Scoped proposal

Within 3–5 days you get a written proposal with fixed scope, fixed price, defined deliverables, and a delivery timeline. No mystery line items, no "to be determined" sections, no hourly rates that drift.

If anything in the proposal doesn't fit — scope, timing, budget — we adjust before you sign. The contract reflects what was agreed; nothing else.

[01]Pricing & engagement

Pricing is fixed per engagement and quoted in the proposal after the discovery call. Audits and sprints are priced as a flat fee for the entire scope. Fractional retainers are priced per month with a capped hour commitment.

I don't publish a price list because the variance between engagements is significant — a HubSpot audit for a 15-person SaaS company and one for a 120-person manufacturer are different exercises. What I will commit to: every proposal has a fixed price, no hourly drift, and no surprises mid-engagement.

Hourly billing rewards slow work. Fixed-fee aligns my incentive with yours: deliver the outcome efficiently. You know your total cost before you sign. I'm motivated to be fast and accurate.

If we discover something during the engagement that warrants additional work — a problem the audit surfaces that wasn't in scope, for example — we discuss it explicitly. You decide whether to add a sprint, defer it, or drop it. Nothing gets added to your bill without a signed change order.
